GROWTH EXCELLENCE

My Modeling & Innovation Journey

Where structural engineering precision meets runway poise and digital community leadership.

Inspired by Structure, Driven by Presence

Originally from the Democratic Republic of the Congo and based in Kampala, Uganda, my path combines Civil Engineering, software development, and multilingual communication (English, French, and Swahili) with fashion modeling.

To me, fashion is living architecture. Every garment has structure, balance, and intention, and a model's responsibility is to bring that design to life with discipline, posture, and authenticity.

Gaining Runway Experience at Mr. IUEA 2026

Competing in Mr. IUEA 2026 at the International University of East Africa was my breakthrough arena in professional modeling. Stepping onto the runway taught me stage choreography, live audience engagement, wardrobe transitions, and how to project calm confidence under bright lights.

I entered Mr. IUEA 2026 with a clear conviction: a modern model and leader should deliver lasting value beyond the stage. That commitment inspired me to engineer IUEA Connect.

Gabriel Mukamba Mr. IUEA 2026 Contestant • Founder of IUEA Connect
